Look, preview, act

That is the whole workflow. Nothing is deleted until you add -Force.
Step 1 · look
Scan
Measure every known bloat location and print a table. Read-only.
> cguard scan
Step 2 · preview
Clean (dry run)
Shows what would be reclaimed and names the biggest single win.
> cguard clean
Step 3 · act
Clean -Force
Reclaims cache and app tiers. Your documents, repos and settings are never in scope.
> cguard clean -Force
Why it is safe
Three tiers. review tier is measured, never deleted.
Databases survive
A keep list protects files like opencode.db inside cleaned folders.
Agents keep working
minAgeDays leaves anything written recently alone.
Stop the regrowth
relocate moves package and browser caches to another drive.
